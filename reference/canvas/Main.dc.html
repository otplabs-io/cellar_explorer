The family

The domaine dates to 1959, when Jacques Gagnard married Marie-Josèphe Delagrange. Most of the vines later passed to their daughters, Laurence and Claudine, of Fontaine-Gagnard and Blain-Gagnard. Grandson Marc-Antonin Blain makes the small remaining core in a traditional style.

The vintage

2019 was warm and dry. Spring frost and poor flowering cut the crop, and what survived came in ripe and concentrated. Whites turned out rich, with good balance and freshness.
