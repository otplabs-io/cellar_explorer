## Conflicts

- Production: producer sheet 80,000 bottles; merchant citing a critic 75,000.
- Graciano: Decanter 3%; producer says only 'the rest' for Mazuelo and Graciano; Enbotella shows percentages that sum to 105%.
- Tondonia planting: 1913 to 1914 (producer) vs 1914 (Haro tourism) vs replanting 1901 to 1907 (Decanter snippet).
- Cellar barrels: about 12,900 (producer home page) vs about 15,000 (Haro tourism).
- Window: Decanter 2021 to 2024, Atkin and Wine Advocate to 2028, CellarTracker 2020 to 2027.
- Bosconia altitude: 465 m headline vs a 460 to 487 m range on the same page.
- White and rosado Crianza total time: the Consejo page gives only 6 months in oak. Secondary sources say 18 months total.
- Vineyard area: more than 66,000 ha (Consejo), 66,405 ha (2025 report via search summary), 65,326 ha (Wikipedia, 2016/17 data).
- Oriental area: about 25,200 ha versus about 24,600 ha (Decanter 2023).
- New classification dates: Viñedo Singular is 2017 (Consejo). Wikipedia gives 2018 for the wider village and site rules.
