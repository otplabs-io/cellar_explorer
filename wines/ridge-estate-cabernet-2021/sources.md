## Conflicts

- Alcohol: Ridge says 13.3%, some merchants list 14%. Ridge used.
- Oak: one search summary of a merchant page said about 75% new oak. Ridge's tech sheet says 50% new. Ridge used, merchant page not read.
- Wikipedia's infobox gives annual rainfall of 3 inches, while its text says rain is much greater than nearby areas. Rainfall not used.
- Wikipedia says about 480,000 acres. Another source seen in search said about 322,000. Acreage not used.
- Established: 4 December 1981 (Wikipedia, Ridge says 1981) against January 1982 in one search snippet.
